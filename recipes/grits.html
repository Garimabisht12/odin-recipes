Breakfast grits

Looking for Southern grits recipes? Allrecipes has more than 60 recipes for grits, cheese grits, and baked grits complete with ratings, reviews and cooking tips.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup hominy grits
  • 1/2 cup shredded sharp Cheddar cheese
  • 1 teaspoon butter
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Water

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Bring water and salt to a boil in a saucepan. Whisk in grits; continue to whisk for 1 minute.
  3. When the mixture comes to a boil again, turn the heat to low. Stir frequently, cook for 10 to 15 minutes.
  4. Remove grits from the heat and add cheese, butter, and pepper; mix well.
  5. Stir until butter and cheese are melted.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
